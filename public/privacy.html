Surya Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Surya is a voice-driven, location-triggered reminder app. This policy explains what data the app handles and why. We aim to collect as little as possible and to keep location processing on your device.

Who we are

Surya ("we", "us") provides the Surya mobile application. For any privacy question, contact us at maruthiphani75@gmail.com.

Information we process

  • Account. When you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive a user identifier (and, depending on the provider, your email and display name) to create your account.
  • Your reminders. The reminder text, the place name or chain/category you choose, an optional radius, and reminder status are stored in your account so they sync across your devices.
  • Location. Your device's location is used on the device to detect when you arrive at a reminder's place and to find nearby branches of a chain/category. We do not continuously track or store your location history on our servers. Geofence evaluation happens on your device.
  • Voice. Speech you dictate is converted to text by your device's speech recognition. Only the resulting text (never raw audio) may be sent to our server for parsing when the on-device parser cannot handle it.
  • Push token. A device messaging token is stored so we can send you notifications.

How we use third parties

  • Google Firebase — authentication, database sync, and push notifications.
  • Google Places — to find places and nearby branches for your reminders.
  • Google Gemini — to parse reminder text only when needed (the on-device parser handles most commands without it).
  • RevenueCat / app stores — to manage optional Pro subscriptions. We do not receive your full payment details.

When reminder text is sent for parsing, we remove the "Hey <assistant name>" prefix first. We do not sell your personal data.

Data retention

Your reminders and profile are kept until you delete them or delete your account. You can remove individual reminders in the app at any time.

Your choices

  • You control location and notification permissions in your device settings.
  • You can delete reminders in the app, and request account deletion by emailing us.

Children

Surya is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
