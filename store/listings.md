## Play Store (Google)

**Short description (≤80 chars)**
> Speak a reminder; Surya notifies you the moment you arrive at the place.

**Full description**
> Surya is a voice-driven, location-triggered reminder app.
>
> Tell Surya what to remember and where — "remind me to buy milk at Walmart" — and it notifies you when you arrive. Most commands are understood on your device, so it's fast and works offline for simple reminders.
>
> Features:
> • Voice or text input, with on-device understanding
> • Specific places (one location) or dynamic chains/categories (any nearby branch)
> • Efficient geofencing — no continuous GPS tracking
> • Syncs across your devices with your account
> • Free tier: 5 active reminders. Pro: unlimited.
>
> Note: reminders trigger by location today; time-based scheduling is coming.

**Data safety (summary to declare in Play Console)**
- Location: used in-app for geofencing; not shared; processed on device.
- Personal info (email/name from sign-in): account management.
- App activity (reminders): app functionality, synced to your account.
- Messaging token: push notifications.
